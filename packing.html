Packing Basics

Packing smart is not just about what fits in your bag. It is about being prepared for the unexpected. From weather delays and holiday crowds to airline strikes or sudden cancellations, every trip has the potential for hiccups. In unexpected situations, it is better to have it and not need it, than to need it and not have it. Having the right essentials on hand means you will be ready to adjust your plans, manage long waits, and even handle compensation or re-booking situations with less stress. Families especially benefit from thoughtful packing, as it can turn potential nightmares into manageable inconveniences.

Clothing

Outfits, layers, sleepwear.
  • Base layers: underwear, socks (1–2 extra pairs)
  • Tops: tees, 1–2 nicer shirts, sweater/fleece
  • Bottoms: jeans/chinos + lightweight pants/shorts
  • Sleepwear & loungewear
  • Outerwear based on climate (rain jacket / coat)
  • Footwear: walking shoes + dressier option; flip-flops for showers/beach
  • Tip: roll clothes or use packing cubes to save space

Documents

Passport, copies, visas.
  • Passport + 2 photocopies (stored separately)
  • Driver’s license / ID card
  • Visa/ESTA approvals (if required)
  • Travel insurance confirmation
  • Itinerary & booking confirmations (printed + digital)
  • Emergency contacts & embassy info
  • Tip: keep essentials in a waterproof pouch

Tech

Chargers, power bank, adapters.
  • Phone + charger + power bank (10,000–20,000 mAh)
  • Universal travel adapter + spare cable
  • Headphones/earbuds (noise-cancelling if possible)
  • eSIM/roaming plan details
  • Optional: camera + SD cards; laptop/tablet only if needed
  • Tip: pack a small 3-outlet cube for hotel rooms

Toiletries

Medications, TSA liquids, kit.
  • Toothbrush/paste, deodorant, comb/brush
  • Liquids ≤ 3.4 oz (100 ml) in a quart-size bag (carry-on)
  • Razor (disposable/safety per airline rules)
  • Prescription medications + copies of scripts
  • Basic first-aid: bandages, pain reliever, motion sickness
  • Tip: solids (bar soap/shampoo) to save liquid allowance

Food & Drink

Snacks, refillable water bottle, gum.
  • Reusable, empty water bottle (fill after security)
  • Non-messy snacks: granola bars, nuts, jerky, crackers
  • Electrolyte packets for long flights/hot climates
  • Gum or lozenges (ear pressure, freshen breath)
  • Tip: check destination rules for bringing food across borders

Accessories

Sunglasses, hats, travel pillow.
  • Daypack / small cross-body for daily outings
  • Sunglasses, hat/cap, compact umbrella
  • Travel pillow + light eye mask/earplugs
  • Compression socks for long flights
  • Small laundry kit: sink stopper, travel detergent, clothesline
  • Tip: lightweight scarf = warmth + sun cover + style

Quick Tips 🎒

  • Roll clothes to save space.

  • Keep medications in carry-on.
  • Keep toiletries in clear bags.
  • Leave room for souvenirs.
