Seasonal Travel Guide

Travel to the right places at the right time.

Recommendations will align with your selected timezone from the homepage.

Seasonal Travel Details

❄️ Winter (December to February)

Best for first-time travelers: The Caribbean, Thailand, and the Canary Islands.
Use caution: Northern Canada and Siberia because of extreme cold.

The Caribbean (Aruba, Curaçao, Barbados)

  • The weather is typically dry and warm, with temperatures around 77 to 88°F (25 to 31°C). Sea conditions are comfortable for swimming and snorkeling.
  • Some islands are flat and formed by coral, while others are volcanic and more mountainous. Both offer beautiful beaches and clear water.
  • Popular sights include Eagle Beach in Aruba, Harrison’s Cave in Barbados, and views of the Pitons in Saint Lucia.
  • This season is popular and prices can be higher. Book flights and hotels early and consider travel insurance to protect your plans.

Thailand (Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket and Krabi)

  • The weather is drier and slightly cooler, with temperatures around 68 to 90°F (20 to 32°C). Beach conditions are generally sunny and the sea is calmer.
  • Major holidays can bring large crowds. Reserve trains, island ferries, and popular tours ahead of time.
  • Top experiences include the Grand Palace in Bangkok, the Old City temples and night markets in Chiang Mai, and the beaches around Railay and the Phi Phi Islands.
  • Dress modestly when visiting temples. In northern Thailand, bring a light layer for cooler evenings.

The Canary Islands (Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Lanzarote)

  • Expect mild temperatures of about 61 to 72°F (16 to 22°C) with frequent sunshine and ocean breezes.
  • The islands are volcanic, with black-sand beaches, lava fields, and dramatic landscapes. Mount Teide on Tenerife is a highlight.
  • Popular activities include the Mount Teide cable car, Timanfaya National Park on Lanzarote, and the dunes near Maspalomas on Gran Canaria.

Places that are difficult in winter

  • Northern Canada and Siberia can be extremely cold with limited daylight. Transportation and outdoor activities may be restricted and specialized clothing is required.

🌸 Spring (March to May)

Best for first-time travelers: Japan, Paris, and the deserts of the American Southwest.
Use caution: Parts of Southeast Asia that experience flooding.

Japan (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka)

  • Typical temperatures range from about 50 to 70°F (10 to 21°C). Cherry blossom timing varies by region, often from late March to early April in many cities.
  • This season is very popular. Reserve rail passes, hotels, and timed museum entries as early as possible.
  • Highlights include Ueno Park and the Meguro River in Tokyo, the Philosopher’s Path and Fushimi Inari Shrine in Kyoto, and Osaka Castle.
  • Be respectful during flower viewing and at temples and shrines. Follow posted guidance and local customs.

Paris and Northern France

  • Expect temperatures around 45 to 65°F (7 to 18°C). Light rain is common, so bring a compact umbrella or rain jacket.
  • Lines are usually shorter than in summer, and many café terraces begin to open.
  • Top sights include the Louvre, the Musée d’Orsay, the Notre-Dame area, Montmartre, and the Palace of Versailles on a day trip.

The American Southwest (Arizona and Utah)

  • Daytime temperatures often range from 60 to 85°F (16 to 29°C), which is ideal for hiking before the intense summer heat.
  • Well-known places include the Grand Canyon, Zion National Park, Bryce Canyon, Antelope Canyon, and Horseshoe Bend.
  • Carry water and sun protection. Some parks require shuttles or timed entry, so check requirements in advance.

Places that are difficult in spring

  • Some parts of Southeast Asia experience heavy rain and flooding that can disrupt transportation and outdoor plans. Review local weather patterns before booking.

☀️ Summer (June to August)

Best for first-time travelers: Greece, the Alps, and Hawaii.
Use caution: Countries along the Persian Gulf because of extreme heat, and parts of India during peak heat and monsoon.

Greece (Cyclades Islands such as Santorini, Mykonos, Naxos, and Paros)

  • Weather is hot and dry, often between 77 and 95°F (25 to 35°C). Strong seasonal winds can affect ferry schedules.
  • July and August are very busy. Book ferries and hotels early, or consider less crowded islands such as Naxos, Paros, or Milos.
  • Do not miss the Acropolis in Athens, sunset views in Oia on Santorini, and the many beaches and local tavernas across the islands.

The Alps (Switzerland, Austria, France, and Italy)

  • Valleys often see temperatures of 60 to 77°F (16 to 25°C), with cooler conditions at higher elevations. This is a great time for hiking and panoramic train rides.
  • Highlights include Jungfraujoch and Schilthorn in Switzerland, Hallstatt and Salzburg in Austria, Chamonix and Mont Blanc in France, and the Dolomites in Italy.
  • Afternoon storms can occur. Begin hikes early and reserve popular cable cars in busy areas.

Hawaii (Oahu, Maui, Hawaii Island, and Kauai)

  • Summer is generally dry, with temperatures around 75 to 88°F (24 to 31°C). Ocean conditions are good for swimming and snorkeling.
  • Highlights include Diamond Head on Oahu, the Road to Hana on Maui, Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park on Hawaii Island, and the Na Pali Coast on Kauai.
  • Car rentals and park reservations can sell out. Book them in advance.

Places that are difficult in summer

  • Countries along the Persian Gulf, such as the United Arab Emirates and Qatar, can be extremely hot and limit daytime activities.
  • In parts of India, heat and monsoon rains can be intense. Consider cooler regions in the mountains for better conditions.

🍂 Fall (September to November)

Best for first-time travelers: New England, Italy, and Japan during autumn leaves.
Use caution: Areas that frequently experience hurricanes in the Caribbean and the southeastern United States.

New England (Vermont, New Hampshire, and Maine)

  • Early fall often brings temperatures around 50 to 70°F (10 to 21°C). It becomes cooler by late October.
  • Peak foliage usually occurs from late September to mid October depending on latitude and elevation. Check current foliage reports when planning.
  • Popular drives and hikes include the Kancamagus Highway, Acadia National Park, and the areas around the towns of Stowe and Smugglers’ Notch.

Italy (Rome, Florence and Tuscany, the Amalfi Coast, and Puglia)

  • Temperatures often range from 60 to 77°F (16 to 25°C). Crowds are smaller than in summer and the weather is comfortable for walking.
  • Highlights include the Colosseum and the Vatican Museums in Rome, the Uffizi Gallery and the Cathedral in Florence, coastal scenery on the Amalfi Coast, and seasonal harvest festivals in wine regions.

Japan during autumn leaves

  • Conditions are crisp and comfortable, with temperatures around 55 to 72°F (13 to 22°C).
  • Leaves often change color from late October through late November depending on the location. Kyoto’s temples and the national parks near Nikko are especially beautiful.
  • Reserve popular sites ahead of time and bring a light layer for cooler evenings.

Places that are difficult in fall

  • Hurricane season runs from June through November. Storms can still occur in the fall. Consider flexible bookings and travel insurance if you are visiting higher-risk areas.
