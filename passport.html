Passport Steps

1. Eligibility & ID

Confirm first-time status; gather proof of citizenship & photo ID.
  • First-time applicants, minors, and anyone who can’t use renewal by mail must apply in person with Form DS-11.
  • Proof of U.S. citizenship (one of):
    • Certified U.S. birth certificate (with registrar’s seal/file date)
    • Consular Report of Birth Abroad (FS-240)
    • Naturalization Certificate or Certificate of Citizenship
  • Photo ID (one of): valid driver’s license, state ID, military ID, or current/valid government ID.
  • Photocopies: Bring a legible photocopy of the front and back of each ID/citizenship document (on white 8.5×11” paper).
  • Name changes: If your current name differs from your citizenship/ID documents, bring legal proof (marriage certificate, court order).

2. Photo

2×2” color photo, white background, neutral expression, no glasses.
  • Specs: 2×2 inch color, taken within 6 months, plain white/off-white background. Face centered; neutral expression or natural smile.
  • No glasses (medical exceptions require a signed doctor’s note). No hats/head coverings (religious/medical exceptions allowed with statement).
  • Where to get photos taken:
    • Pharmacies/Retail: Walgreens, CVS, Rite Aid
    • Shipping/Office: UPS Store, FedEx Office, Staples, Office Depot
    • Post Offices: many USPS acceptance facilities offer photo service
    • AAA offices, local camera/photo studios
  • Official Photo Requirements

3. Form

Complete online & print or handwrite in black ink. Don’t sign yet.
  • DS-11 (Apply in Person): First-time adult applicants, all minors, lost/stolen passports, or when you don’t meet mail-in renewal rules.
  • DS-82 (Renew by Mail): Adult renewal if your most recent passport is undamaged, not lost/stolen, issued when you were 16+, issued ≤15 years ago, and you use the same name (or provide legal proof of name change).
  • DS-64: Lost or stolen passport
  • DS-3053: Notarized consent for a minor when one parent/guardian cannot appear.
  • DS-5525: Statement of exigent/special family circumstances (when consent from a parent/guardian for a minor can’t be obtained).
  • DS-5504: Name change/data errors/limited validity corrections soon after issuance (eligibility/time limits apply).

Fill Out DS-11 Online (External)

  • Do not sign DS-11 until the acceptance agent instructs you.
  • Use black ink if handwriting; verify names, DOB, contact info, and SSN are accurate.
  • Print single-sided; bring the full form to your appointment.

4. Appointment

Apply at an acceptance facility (post office, clerk of court, etc.).
  • Find a location: Use the State Department’s locator to find acceptance facilities near you.
  • Scheduling: Many USPS locations require appointments; some courts/local offices accept walk-ins. Arrive 10–15 minutes early.
  • Bring: DS-11 (unsigned), citizenship proof + copy, photo ID + copy, 1 compliant photo, and acceptable payments (often two separate payments).

5. Fees

Application fee (State) + execution fee (facility); optional expedite & delivery.
  • Two payments are common:
    • Application Fee — paid to: U.S. Department of State (typically by check or money order only made payable exactly to “U.S. Department of State”).
    • Execution/Acceptance Fee — paid to the facility (varies by site: some accept card, cash, or money order).
  • Extras (optional): Expedited service fee; 1–2 day delivery return shipping (paid to State); overnight shipping to the agency (paid at the facility/carrier).
  • Amounts change: Always verify current totals before your appointment.

6. Submit

Sign in front of the agent; hand in DS-11, photo, IDs/copies, and fees.
  • At the counter: The acceptance agent will instruct you to sign DS-11, review your documents, and witness your oath/signature.
  • Documents sent: Your citizenship evidence usually gets mailed to the State Department and returned separately from your passport.
  • Receipt: Keep it; it may include key details for status tracking.

7. Track & Receive

Check status online; allow extra days for mailing after processing.
  • Status online: It can take up to 2 weeks after you apply for the application to appear in the system, then you can track it.
  • Mailing: Your new passport and your original documents typically arrive in separate envelopes.
  • Urgent travel: If you have imminent international travel, contact the National Passport Information Center or review urgent appointment options.

Common Mistakes ✅

  • Using DS-82 instead of DS-11
  • Wrong photo size/background
  • Signing before meeting the agent
  • Submitting copies instead of originals
  • Booking travel before passport arrives
